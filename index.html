01Profile

I’m a product executive who came up the quantitative side of fintech. Over eight years at one company I went from pricing trading instruments to running product — and in between I built the monetization function from nothing: the pricing and risk behind every instrument we trade, the economics of the partner network that acquires users worldwide, and the loyalty and retention systems that keep them.

But the real heavy-lifting was never the math — it was the org: building and reshaping teams, the hard calls about who leads what, and moving a department to a new way of working when the old way felt safe. Most of that gets done by winning over people you don’t control — not by telling anyone what to do.

What I bring to the CPO seat isn’t the modeling — it’s the judgment the whole climb bought: I can see which product bets actually compound in the economics, not just which ones demo well.

04How I lead

Build the team, then make yourself unnecessary.

I’d rather turn a reluctant expert into a leader who takes the role seriously than hand the title to whoever wants it most.

Management is a craft you can teach — if the person wants to learn it.

The best engineer gets promoted to lead and thinks the hard part is behind them. It isn’t.

Cheap and fast beats big and slow.

I moved a product org off measure-everything dogma toward fast, evidence-first bets — and when the bottleneck moved, I moved the org again.

The constraint isn’t shipping — it’s decision quality.

Anyone can build fast now. The edge is deciding well, and biasing toward bets you can reverse.

Hard calls, handled like a system.

Protect continuity, sequence the communication, act decisively — never in anger.
